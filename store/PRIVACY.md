# Bro: privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

Bro is a joke Chrome extension. It adds ", bro" to the end of sentences on the web pages you visit.

## What it reads

To do that, Bro reads the text of each page you open, in your browser. It skips form fields, code, and anything you can type into.

## What it keeps

One setting: the bro level you picked (Normal, Ultra or Super duper hyper cyber bro). It is saved with Chrome's own storage, so Chrome may sync it to your other computers if you have Chrome sync turned on.

## What it sends

Nothing. Bro has no server. It never sends page text, browsing history or anything else anywhere, and it contains no analytics, ads or trackers.
